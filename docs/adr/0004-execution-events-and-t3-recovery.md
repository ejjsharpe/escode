# Central Execution authority with retained events and T3 recovery policy

Accepted direction, 2026-10-31, through [Resolve execution retention, cutover and recovery guarantees](https://github.com/ejjsharpe/escode/issues/159): consolidate host orchestration in Effect and commit execution events, normalized content, projections, receipts and required follow-up jobs together, so clients and recovery share one authority. Adopt the pinned T3 retention and restart policy and a fresh store, trading the earlier current-state-only/provider-history approach for retained local content and rebuildable shared projections without pretending that external effects join the database transaction. Keep WAL `NORMAL`: copying T3's retained-state guards accepts undetectable control-record rollback after OS/power loss, rather than adding the proposed same-boot restriction or a stronger control journal.

The [specification amendment](../specifications/execution-architecture.md) defines the exact reference, retention, limits, cutover and qualification. It supersedes the execution-storage and blanket no-continuation choices in [ADR 0002](0002-versioned-recovery-and-history.md) and [ADR 0003](0003-sqlite-and-turn-checkpoints.md); native context, file checkpoints, independent client recovery and uncertainty safeguards remain. This is planning, not implementation acceptance.
